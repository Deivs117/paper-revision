\revblue{In simulation, an ablation study showed that removing the circuit's lateral inhibition or its STN/STR layers reduced success under stimulus conflict from 100\% to 25\% and 33\%, respectively. Against a priority-rule finite state machine (FSM) baseline under matched conditions, the neural arbitration succeeded in every three-stimulus conflict trial, where the FSM succeeded in none, and reached 85\% success versus 45\% in an inspection task with an initially hidden target.}
